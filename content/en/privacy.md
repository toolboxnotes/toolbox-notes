# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 9, 2026_

Toolbox Notes ("this site") respects your privacy. This page explains what information this site handles and how.

## Information we collect

This site has no accounts or comments and does not directly collect personal information such as your name or email address.

## Browser storage

This site stores a few preferences in your browser's local storage — your chosen language, light/dark mode, and theme color — so it can remember them on your next visit. They are never sent to a server.

## Advertising (Google AdSense)

This site may display ads served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other websites.

You can opt out of personalized advertising in [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com). See [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads) for details.

## Hosting and analytics

This site is served by Cloudflare, which may process basic connection data such as IP addresses for security and delivery.

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use it, such as which pages are viewed, how visitors arrive, and roughly which country and device they use. Google Analytics uses cookies and does not give this site your name or email address. You can opt out with the [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). See [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) for details.

## Emails you send us

If you email [toolbox@toolboxnotes.com](mailto:toolbox@toolboxnotes.com), your address and message are used only to reply to you and are never shared with third parties.
