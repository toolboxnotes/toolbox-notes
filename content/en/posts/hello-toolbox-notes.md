Every so often a task comes along that is just annoying enough to repeat — copying numbers between sheets, checking the same pages every morning, renaming a pile of files. At some point it becomes faster to build a small tool than to keep doing it by hand.

**Toolbox Notes** is where I write those tools down.

## What you'll find here

The topics won't be limited to one field. If I built something because I needed it, it can end up here. For each tool I'll try to cover:

- **The problem** — what was slow, error-prone, or tedious before
- **What the tool does** — the main features, with screenshots
- **How to use it** — enough detail that you could try the same approach
- **What I learned** — the mistakes and surprises along the way
