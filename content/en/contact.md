# Contact

For questions about a post, corrections, or inquiries about the programs featured here, email us at:

**[toolbox@toolboxnotes.com](mailto:toolbox@toolboxnotes.com)**

## What to write to us about

- **SellerMorning** — if you're interested in the Amazon seller management program or would like to try it
- **The posts** — anything unclear, Amazon policies or screens that have changed, or mistakes you spotted
- **Ideas for future posts** — topics you'd like covered or tools you'd like to see

## When you email

- For SellerMorning inquiries, it helps to mention your marketplace (for example Amazon.com), how many products you manage, and which daily task takes you the most time.
- **Never send** your seller account password, API keys or payment details by email. We will never ask for them.
- We usually reply within two to three business days.

Your email address and message are used only to reply to you. See our [Privacy Policy](../privacy/) for details.
